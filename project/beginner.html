LEGO Beginner Guide

Getting started with LEGO can be exciting, but the large number of available sets and themes can also make it difficult to decide where to begin. This guide provides some simple advice to help new builders get started.

Choosing Your First LEGO Set

When choosing your first LEGO set, start by thinking about what you enjoy. LEGO has themes based on movies, games, vehicles, fantasy worlds, buildings, and many other interests.

You do not need to start with a large or expensive set. A smaller set can be a great way to learn how LEGO instructions work and discover what types of builds you enjoy.

Five Tips for New Builders

1. Start Small

Begin with a set that fits your experience level and budget. Smaller sets can help you become comfortable with the building process.

2. Choose Something You Like

Pick a theme that interests you. Building is usually more enjoyable when you are excited about the finished model.

3. Follow the Instructions

Take your time and carefully follow each step. If you make a mistake, do not be afraid to go back and find where the problem occurred.

4. Organize Your Pieces

Keeping LEGO pieces organized can make building easier and help you find the pieces you need.

5. Have Fun

LEGO is a creative hobby. You do not have to build everything perfectly or follow instructions all the time.

Building on a Budget

LEGO sets come in many different sizes and prices. New collectors should consider setting a budget before purchasing a set.

One good strategy is to compare prices from different stores before buying. Sales and promotions can also be useful for finding sets at a lower price.

Remember that a higher price does not always mean that a set will be more enjoyable. Choose sets based on the themes and building experiences you enjoy.

Starting a Collection

Some fans enjoy building sets and taking them apart, while others prefer displaying their completed models. There is no wrong way to enjoy the hobby.

If you decide to collect LEGO, consider choosing a few themes that you enjoy rather than trying to collect everything. This can help keep your collection organized and your spending under control.

LEGO Storage Ideas

Small Containers

Small containers can be useful for separating commonly used LEGO pieces by type or color.

Drawer Organizers

Drawer organizers can help keep large collections organized while making individual pieces easy to access.

Display Shelves

Shelves provide a way to display completed sets while keeping them organized and easy to see.

Ready to Start?

Before purchasing your first set, consider these questions:

  • What LEGO themes interest me?
  • What is my budget?
  • How much space do I have for LEGO?
  • Do I want to build, display, or collect?
  • What size set would I enjoy building?

Once you have answer these questions, you will have a better idea of what type of LEGO set would be a good starting point.
